How the figures are calculated

Purchase value (certified) = Total Cost of each supplier certificate (Landed Cost + Other Additions − Other Deductions), incl. GST.   Paid = settled against the invoice: cash paid + advance adjusted + TDS + debit notes.   Outstanding = processed by Finance, not yet paid (ties to the Finance Supplier Outstanding report).   In approval = Submitted / Approved certificates not yet processed.   Yet to be paid = Outstanding + In approval + approved advances not yet released.   For every filter: Purchase value = Paid + Outstanding + In approval (+ retention, if any). A certificate raised against several POs is split across those POs in proportion to each PO's net value, so nothing is counted twice. Supplier advances are shown separately and are not added to purchase value (the invoice that recovers them already carries the full value). Office purchases (Office, CBE Office, MP Guindy Office, PVT Head Office) are excluded — this dashboard covers project purchases only.
Engineering work orders: the work types finalised and paid by Purchase (Lift, Grill, UPVC, Carpenter, CCTV, Borewell, STP, Signage, Hand Rail, and the rest of the agreed list) are taken from the ENGG certificate report. Their Running / Final bills add to purchase value; Adhoc certificates are advances, shown with the advances and recovered on the bills; Retention certificates release retention already held, so they are not new value. Use the Source filter to see PO or work-order figures alone. The Finance outstanding cross-check covers Purchase certificates only.
